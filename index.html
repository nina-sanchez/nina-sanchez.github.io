About Casa Regina

This place is close to our hearts, and we’ve poured a lot of love into making it a welcoming retreat for family, friends, and fellow travelers. Our goal was to create a space that feels like a home away from home — a place where you can unwind, recharge, and still be close to everything that makes Tulum so special.

With the help of our family in Tulum, we’ve sourced locally crafted furniture, artwork, and décor to preserve the authentic charm of the region. We combined that with the comforts of home, creating a perfect spot for beach days, jungle adventures, cenote swims, Mayan ruins, or just relaxing on the rooftop. We’re always here if you need anything or have questions — we want your stay to be as relaxing and memorable as possible.
